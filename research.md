# Lumora — Research Notes

Grounding for the core claims of the product. Every design decision should
trace to one of these findings.

## Retrieval practice & the testing effect

- Retrieving information from memory produces stronger long-term retention than
  re-studying (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006; Karpicke & Blunt, 2011 — practice
  retrieval beat concept mapping for learning).
- **Design consequence:** quizzes are generated by default for every lesson;
  the study flow is lesson → active recall, not lesson → re-read.

## Spacing & the spacing effect

- Distributed practice reliably outperforms massed practice (Cepeda et al.,
  2006 meta-analysis).
- Optimal review occurs just before the point of forgetting — the forgetting
  curve (Ebbinghaus, 1885) is flattened by strategic review timing.
- **Design consequence:** SM-2 (SuperMemo 2) with modern modifications
  (hard rating, ease floor, first-interval ladder) schedules every card and
  every concept review.

## Desirable difficulty

- Conditions that make practice feel harder — but remain successful — improve
  long-term retention (Bjork & Bjork, 2011).
- Interleaving topics feels harder yet improves discrimination between problem
  types (Rohrer & Taylor, 2007).
- **Design consequence:** the engine generates mixed question types and the
  study experience makes mistakes feel productive (feedback + traceability).

## Levels of processing

- Deep, meaning-based processing produces better memory than shallow processing
  (Craik & Lockhart, 1972).
- **Design consequence:** concept definitions and explanations are always tied
  to *meaning* — the source sentence — never isolated trivia.

## Metacognition & calibration

- Learners overestimate how well they know material after re-reading
  (Koriat & Bjork, 2005).
- **Design consequence:** measurable mastery (per quiz, concept, module) and
  visible weak areas replace self-reported confidence.

## Source-grounded generation (trust)

- LLMs hallucinate; grounding answers in retrieved passages with citations is
  the standard mitigation (Gao et al., 2023; Lewis et al., 2020 RAG).
- **Design consequence:** every generated item carries `sourceRef` +
  `sourceChunkId`; the local engine structurally cannot fabricate because it
  only rewrites the source.

## Competitor landscape (summary)

| Product | Model | Gap Lumora targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NotebookLM | Summarization/chat over sources | No structured curriculum, no SRS, no mastery |
| Anki | Pure SRS | No document→curriculum, no source grounding |
| Quizlet | Flashcards/quizzes | No curriculum, no traceability, shallow SRS |
| Generic ChatGPT | Unstructured chat | No retention system at all |

## Open questions for later phases

- Optimal default question mix (MCQ vs short-answer) for retention — the
